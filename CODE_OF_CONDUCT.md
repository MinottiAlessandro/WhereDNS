# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and empathetic.
- Focus criticism on ideas and technical evidence rather than people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize for mistakes, and learn from experience.
- Respect privacy, authorization boundaries, and responsible disclosure.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, publication of private information, and misuse of project spaces for unauthorized security activity are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile contact methods. The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject contributions or participation that violates this code.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).
